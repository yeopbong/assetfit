# Third-party notices

Sample assets and their derivatives retain the attribution and licenses in [examples/SOURCES.json](examples/SOURCES.json). The Duck model's license is [examples/sources/Duck-license.txt](examples/sources/Duck-license.txt). Deliveries include the applicable source manifest and license text.

Dependency copyright and license files accompany package installations. The browser build includes their full notices in `dist/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`; JSZip uses its MIT license option.
